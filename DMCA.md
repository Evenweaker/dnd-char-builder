3. Identification of the material that is claimed to be infringing, with enough detail for us to locate it (e.g. file path or URL in this repository)

6. A statement that the information in the notification is accurate, and under penalty of perjury, that you are authorized to act on behalf of the copyright owner

If your material was removed and you believe that was a mistake, you may send a counter-notice to the same email with the information required under applicable law (including identification of the removed material, your contact details, consent to local jurisdiction where applicable, and a good-faith statement that the material was removed by mistake).
